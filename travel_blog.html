My day in Paris

On my first full morning, I set an alarm for 6:00 AM. Walking out onto the platform at Place du Trocadéro just as dawn broke meant having the view of the Eiffel Tower almost completely to myself. The sky shifted from deep indigo to pale pink, framing the wrought-iron giant without a single tour bus in sight. After the light show ended, I bypassed the tourist cafes and ducked into a quiet boulangerie two blocks away. Ordering a still-warm pain au chocolat and taking that first flaky, buttery bite while standing on a cobblestone corner felt like the official start of my Parisian life.
